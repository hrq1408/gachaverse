Follow the main story while it unlocks the features you need. Use the region guide and upgrade menus to choose your next activity. When an activity is unavailable, read the conditions in the game before deciding how to proceed.

## Pick a goal for your next session

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The main quest is available | Continue and mark useful travel points for later. |
| An activity or banner is missing | Check Union Level, quest progress, and any alternative route in the official notice. |
| The team survives but fights take too long | Check weapons, the skills you actually use, and your order of switches. |
| You have started collecting Echoes | Compare their effects and cost before spending upgrade materials. |

## Solve the next bottleneck

If your main hit lands after the damage window closes, try starting your setup with a different Resonator. Test dodging or interrupting the enemy before investing materials just to raise the damage number on the character screen.

Kuro's Version 3.8 banner notice illustrates how a Convene may list alternative requirements. Read the notice for your event rather than assuming every activity opens at the same Union Level.
