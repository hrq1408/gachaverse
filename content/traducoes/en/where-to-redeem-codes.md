## Redeem codes on HoYoverse’s websites

HoYoverse maintains code redemption pages for Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, and Zenless Zone Zero. Open the page for your game and follow its instructions.

- [Genshin Impact](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/gift)
- [Honkai: Star Rail](https://hsr.hoyoverse.com/gift)
- [Zenless Zone Zero](https://zenless.hoyoverse.com/redemption)

Before confirming, check the account and, if the page offers those options, the selected server and character.

## Redeem codes in Wuthering Waves

Kuro Games directs players to open the in-game settings, go to Other, and select Redemption Code. After confirming, check your in-game mailbox. [Kuro Games instructions](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/main/news/detail/728)

## Validity and conditions

If the game or redemption page rejects a code, compare each character with the original announcement and check the deadline and eligibility. If redemption succeeds in Wuthering Waves, check your in-game mailbox first. Redeem codes only on the official website or through the in-game menu.

The [code hub](/en/codes/) lists sources and confirmed deadlines. If you’re saving resources for an event, check the [banner calendar](/en/banner-calendar/) for announced dates.
