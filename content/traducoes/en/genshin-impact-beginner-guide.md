Start with the main story and build a team that can handle the next quest. When a quest requires a higher Adventure Rank, open your quest log and map to find activities your account can already access.

## What to do each session

| Situation | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| The story is available | Continue the quest and unlock Teleport Waypoints along the way. |
| The next quest requires a higher rank | Complete available activities from the quest log before exploring distant areas without a goal. |
| Your rank is stuck despite earning experience | Check the quest log for an Adventure Rank Ascension Quest. |
| Wishes are missing from the menu | Check the Adventure Rank requirement in HoYoverse support and continue the prologue. |

## Where to invest first

Start with the character who spends the most time on the field. If a boss takes too long, check that character’s weapon level and the talent you actually use. If you keep getting knocked out, try changing your team before raising everyone’s level.

For example, if you spend most of a Domain fight chasing the enemy, swap to a character who can reach it more reliably or change the order of your abilities. Try the same enemy again before spending upgrade materials.

When you return to the story, choose one concrete improvement, like leveling your main attacker’s weapon. Check the [banner calendar](/en/banner-calendar/) before spending on a Wish, then confirm the end time on your server in the game.
