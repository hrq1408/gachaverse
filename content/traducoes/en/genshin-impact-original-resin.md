Spend Original Resin on the activity that removes your team's next bottleneck. Open the missing material on the upgrade screen and check where it comes from before entering a Domain. Some Domains rotate their materials by day.

## Choose based on what you need now

| Bottleneck | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A character reached a level cap | The Ascension Material and the corresponding boss. |
| A weapon reached a level cap | The Domain and the day its material is available. |
| You can upgrade an important Talent | The required book, the Domain, and any boss material. |
| You have the materials but lack Mora | Blossom of Wealth and rewards from activities you have unlocked. |

## Separate today's jobs from later ones

If a Talent book is available tomorrow, you can spend today's session on an available boss material or items in the world that cost no Resin. Compare the reward cost and the amount you still need before deciding when to stop.

For example, a weapon may need both a Domain material and a common enemy drop. Run the Domain on the correct day and plan a separate route for the enemies. Leave items you already own off the list. The [Genshin Impact hub](/en/games/genshin-impact/) has more progression guides.
