Playing without spending money means making choices. How often you play is up to you. To plan for banners, start by identifying the character you really want and how much you can save before the event ends. Give yourself room to skip releases.

## Set a priority

Decide whether you want to get a favorite character, round out a team, or improve an existing lineup. All three are valid goals. Decide which comes first so that if your budget falls short, you already know where to spend it.

## Separate confirmed resources from estimates

Write down the passes you already have, your available premium currency, and any rewards you have yet to claim. Treat future events as uncertain until their dates and conditions are announced. Leave rumors and supposed codes out of your budget.

## Compare the character and the Light Cone

Ask whether the character works with a Light Cone you already have and whether you have a team that can make use of their abilities. If the team still lacks an essential role, saving for another character may be a better choice. The right call depends on your account.

## Check the official schedule

Read the event notice for the end time, keeping your server and time zone in mind. Start with the [official news page](https://hsr.hoyoverse.com/en-us/news) and rely on confirmed schedules. Give yourself time to decide before the event ends.

## Set a stopping point

Decide how much you’re willing to spend before your first Warp. If you reach that limit without getting what you wanted, stop and reassess. Avoid buying currency to make up for what you’ve already spent. It’s also fine to skip a banner or just play for the story.
