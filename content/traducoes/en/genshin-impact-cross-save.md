To continue your Genshin Impact playthrough on a new device, use the same HoYoverse Account and server you used before. Open the game on the device that still shows your progress before you link any account. That gives you a reference for the correct settings.

## Before switching devices

On the device you already use, open the User Center in the account settings. Note your server and UID so you can compare them later. Keep those details for your own reference and enter passwords or verification codes only in the game or through an official HoYoverse channel.

HoYoverse lists cross-save support for PC, iOS, Android, PlayStation, and Xbox. If the login screen lets you choose a server, select the one you originally played on.

## Linking your account on PlayStation

| Where your progress is saved | First step |
| --- | --- |
| PC or mobile, and you are opening Genshin on PlayStation for the first time | Choose the option to sign in to an existing account and follow the prompts to link your HoYoverse Account. Check first that the PlayStation Network region matches your original server. |
| PlayStation, and you want to play on PC or mobile | Open the account settings on the console and check whether an email is linked in the User Center. If needed, complete the official linking flow before signing in on the new device. |
| Both accounts have progress, or the PSN account is already linked | Check the existing links and contact official support before unlinking or replacing anything. |

## What to check if the game starts over

1. Check the server. On PlayStation, your PSN account region determines it.
2. Check the sign-in method. The Google sign-in button may take you to a different account than typing your Gmail address in the email field. Check which method is linked on your original device.
3. Compare your UID with the one you noted. If your progress still does not appear, contact official support and explain which devices show it.

For example, switching from America to Europe on PC can show a different playthrough. Sign out and select America first. HoYoverse says cross-save does not transfer or merge progress across servers.

## After recovering access

Check that the UID and progress match what you see on your original device. Then return to the [beginner guide in Portuguese](/artigos/genshin-impact-guia-iniciantes/) or check the [Genshin Impact redemption instructions in Portuguese](/jogos/genshin-impact/codigos/).
