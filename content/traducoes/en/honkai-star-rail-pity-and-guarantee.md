Pity is the community term for the limit on Warps without a 5-star. It helps you budget, although it cannot predict an individual Warp.

## Limits by Warp type

| Common Warp type | Warps per 5-star at most |
| --- | ---: |
| Character Event Warp | 90 |
| Light Cone Event Warp | 80 |
| Standard Warp | 90 |

The HoYoverse Help Center lists these limits. The standard pool can give you a character or a Light Cone. Read the details of special formats before spending passes.

## What losing the 50/50 means

On conventional Character Event Warps that follow this rule, the initial chance of directly receiving the featured character applies when a 5-star appears and the featured guarantee is inactive. Depending on the available pool, the featured result may also be in the other outcome. If the result is a different character, the next 5-star in the same compatible event type will be featured. The Help Center lists a different initial featured rate of 75% for Light Cone Event Warps. Check the specific event rules.

## A worst-case budget

If you made 35 Warps since your last 5-star on a standard Character Event Warp format, you need at most 55 more for the next 5-star. Without an active featured guarantee, budget another 90 for a worst case of 145 additional Warps. With the guarantee, the example requires at most 55. These numbers are limits, and your result can arrive sooner.

## Keep counters separate

Standard, Character Event, and Light Cone Event Warps have separate counters. Read the event details to confirm what carries over to a new banner, especially for collaborations or special formats.

## Check your history before spending

Open the history for the matching Warp type and count from the last 5-star. Recent results may take time to appear. Building pity ahead of a desired banner can spend an active guarantee on an early 5-star. Saving passes keeps the choice open. Avoid spending money to make up for resources already spent.

Use the [pity calculator](/ferramentas/calculadora-pity/) and the [Honkai: Star Rail Warp calendar](/jogos/honkai-star-rail/calendario/) in Portuguese. The numbers on the cover are illustrative.
