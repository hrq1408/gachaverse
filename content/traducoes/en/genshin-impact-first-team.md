Use characters you already own to build a team that can land hits reliably and survive long enough to repeat its rotation. Decide who stays on the field, who prepares the target, and who covers the gaps.

## Assign roles before swapping characters

| Role | A quick test |
| --- | --- |
| On-field attacker | Can they land their key hits during the available window? |
| Element application | Does the element remain available after you switch, or do you need to apply it again? |
| Survival | If dodging takes up most of the fight, try a healer or a defensive option. |
| Support | Is the next important ability ready when the rotation calls for it? |

## Change one part of the rotation

Repeat the same opening against an enemy without a permanent elemental aura. Watch how long setup takes and whether your next Elemental Burst is available. If the elemental application ends before the attack lands, move the applicator closer to the attacker in your ability order.

Check the in-game tutorials for the reaction your team relies on. Start with two elements until you can identify who prepares the target and who triggers the reaction. The [Genshin Impact hub](/en/games/genshin-impact/) links to other progression guides and the calendar.
