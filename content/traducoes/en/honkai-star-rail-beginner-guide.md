The first few sessions introduce several currencies, equipment types, and activities. Pick one goal at a time. You can enjoy the main story with the characters and equipment you already have.

## Follow the story until farming opens

Advance the main missions until you unlock material farming. When you find a new activity, try its tutorial and check the rewards. Keep moving through the story before spending time looking for perfect equipment.

## Give each team member a job

Start with a main damage dealer, someone who boosts damage or weakens enemies, someone who keeps the team alive, and a flexible fourth slot. A second support can help more than another attacker if both would need the same Skill Points. When a fight stalls, check damage, survivability, and Skill Point use separately.

## Upgrade what you use

Check each active character's level cap, Light Cone, and the abilities you use in your rotation. Choose one improvement you can finish with your current materials. A usable main team is a better early goal than leveling every new character in parallel.

| When | Next goal |
| --- | --- |
| First sessions | Learn combat and continue the story. |
| After farming unlocks | Pick materials for your main team. |
| A fight stops your progress | Check enemy weaknesses, healing, and action order. |
| End of the first week | Review your inventory and plan the next upgrades. |

This is a suggested order. You can take longer than a week.

## Avoid common resource mistakes

Check which type of pass you need before converting premium currency, read ability descriptions before upgrading, and change your strategy before retrying a difficult fight. If you want a limited character, set a resource budget before opening the Warp menu. The [pity calculator](/en/pity-calculator/) helps you plan for a featured character or Light Cone.
