In the announcement we consulted, Kuro says featured Resonator banners share their count with one another. Featured weapon banners share a different count. Check the rules of the event you plan to use before combining histories.

## Read the Convene terms before using Tides

| Field | What to note |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Resonator, weapon, or a format with special rules. |
| Currency | The announcement uses Radiant Tide for featured Resonators and Forging Tide for featured weapons in these examples. |
| 5-star | In the weapon banners described, the indicated featured weapon is guaranteed when you receive a 5-star weapon. |
| Event change | Check whether the new banner states it belongs to the same guarantee family. |

For example, if you make pulls on the Resonator banner and then plan for the matching weapon, record them on two lines. A thematic connection between the Resonator and the weapon does not merge their counters.

## Check special formats separately

Solsworn Ciphers and Emerald Sentence are featured weapon examples in the Version 3.8 Phase One notice. Those rules apply to the Convene described by Kuro. Check the current rules before using Tides.

For anniversary and rerun formats, read their details and only combine pulls when the in-game history shows they are compatible. The [Wuthering Waves banner calendar in Portuguese](/jogos/wuthering-waves/calendario/) links to the official announcements.
