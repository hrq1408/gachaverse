If your team already works, Customized Synthesis can help fill a specific gear gap. This guide covers the Version 3.5 change and a practical way to decide where to spend Self-Modeling Resin.

## Start with the role of the piece

Compare Cavern Relics and Planar Ornaments separately on the equipment screen. Read each set effect and Main Stat. If a swap helps the character act at the right time or survive the fight, it is worth testing even if the old piece looks better in your inventory.

## Compare stats and sets

Group stats by how much they matter to the character: essential, useful, or low priority. Check the character's ability descriptions. Test two pieces while keeping the rest of the team and gear the same. Look at turn order and consistency throughout the attempt.

## What changed in Customized Synthesis

Version 3.5 merged Wishful Resin into Self-Modeling Resin. The same material is used to select a Main Stat and Subsidiary Stats during synthesis. If an older guide calls for two different types of resin, check whether it predates the change.

## How much resin do selected stats cost?

The HoYoverse Help Center documents these two cases. Check the cost preview in the game before confirming.

| Selected stats | Documented cost |
| --- | --- |
| One Main Stat and one Subsidiary Stat | 2 Self-Modeling Resin |
| One Main Stat and two Subsidiary Stats | 5 Self-Modeling Resin |

The table covers stat selection during synthesis. Enhancement happens later. Judge the new piece by the role it can already fill on your team.

## An example of where to spend resin

Imagine a support character who needs to act before an ally, but your best available piece for that slot lacks the Main Stat you need. First, check whether another piece you own fixes the turn order. If it does not, consider synthesizing a piece with the essential stat. Spend more on Subsidiary Stats when they serve a clear purpose, such as improving survival in a fight you repeat.
