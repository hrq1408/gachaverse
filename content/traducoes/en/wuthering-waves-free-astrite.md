Start by recording your current balance and sorting rewards into three groups: what you can claim now, what you still need to finish, and what depends on an event or requirement. Official announcements describe events that award Astrite, but the content and deadlines vary.

## Where to look during your session

| Situation | How to decide |
| --- | --- |
| You’re short on time | Check rewards for activities you’ve already completed and events that are about to end. |
| The next step is locked | Check an available quest or explore a nearby objective and leave the locked reward out of your estimate. |
| The announcement mentions a return event or invitation | Check that both your account and the other account meet the conditions before estimating the reward. |

## Keep Astrite separate from other currencies

When planning, note which items you can already use for the type of Convene you’re aiming for. Leave out Tides from other categories. If a reward offers a different resource, record it in its own column.

**Example.** An event requires a quest you haven’t started yet. Keep the reward on your list of possible rewards and out of the balance you use to decide whether you can pull on the banner. Check the [Wuthering Waves codes](/jogos/wuthering-waves/codigos/) and look up the banner dates in the [calendar](/jogos/wuthering-waves/calendario/).

Kuro has announced return events that award Astrite. Treat these rewards as conditional, especially when the event requires you to invite another account, and check in-game to see whether you can still participate.
