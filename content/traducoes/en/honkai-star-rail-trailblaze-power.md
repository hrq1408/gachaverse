Trailblaze Power is the energy you use to claim rewards from various activities. Where to spend it depends on which upgrade is holding back your account’s progress. Priorities vary from player to player.

## Check your energy cap

Check the current in-game counter before planning a session, especially after an update. If you intend to use your energy, avoid leaving it sitting for long periods. You can do this without making it a daily obligation.

## Choose an activity based on what’s holding you back

| Problem | Suggested priority |
|---|---|
| Character below the desired level | Character EXP and Credits |
| Level cap reached | Ascension materials |
| An important ability is underleveled | Trace materials |
| Light Cone is too weak | Light Cone EXP and ascension materials |
| Stats don’t fit the character’s needs | Relics and Planar Ornaments |

The upgrade screen shows the specific requirements. Use it to find the activity that gives you the materials you’ll need soon.

## How to plan a farming session

Suppose your damage dealer needs an ability upgrade, but your healer is also too low-level to survive. If survival is keeping you from clearing battles, fix that first. Then go back to upgrading your damage dealer. In this situation, that order is more useful than always investing in your main character first.

## When should you start farming Relics?

Suitable gear already helps during the story. Chasing ideal substats, however, can consume resources without predictable upgrades. Prioritize basic upgrades and step up your farming once your team can clear the relevant activities consistently.

## How to use Fuel

Fuel replenishes energy. Before using it, write down what material you need, how much you already have, and what you plan to upgrade. You can save some Fuel for later. Hold on to your Stellar Jade if you’re planning to Warp.

## Review your needs before spending

Open the character’s details, look at the next upgrade, check your inventory, and choose the activity with the corresponding reward. Stop once you reach your goal. Reassess your priority before choosing an activity for your next session.
