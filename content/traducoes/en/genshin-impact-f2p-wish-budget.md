Start with what you already own. Keep rewards you can still earn in a separate column and leave unannounced income out of the budget.

## Build a three-line budget

| Line | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Current balance | Primogems and the usable Fates for the Wish you intend to make. Keep Intertwined Fates separate from Acquaint Fates. |
| History and guarantee | Wishes after your last 5-star and whether that 5-star was featured, using the relevant banner history. |
| Income before the deadline | Rewards you can actually claim on your server before the banner ends. |

For example, after 68 Character Event Wishes without a 5-star, the 90-Wish limit leaves at most 22 more until a 5-star. Check the featured guarantee separately before assuming those 22 will secure the featured character. Leave untested codes and rewards from events you will miss out of the planned balance.

## Choose where you will stop

Decide whether to stop at any 5-star, the featured character, or a fixed spending cap. Keep spare Primogems unconverted until you know which type of Wish you will use. Check the banner’s Fate icon before counting your attempts.

Compare the [banner calendar](/en/banner-calendar/) and [Genshin Impact codes](/en/codes/genshin-impact/) before assigning a reward to your budget.
