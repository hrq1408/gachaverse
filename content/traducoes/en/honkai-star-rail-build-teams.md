A strong team can take many forms, with or without four rare characters. Your lineup needs to bring individual abilities together into a coherent combat plan. Start with each member's role.

## The four team slots

| Slot | Question to answer |
|---|---|
| Main damage dealer | Who makes the best use of the team's offensive resources? |
| Support | Who boosts that damage or helps the damage dealer land it? |
| Sustain | Who keeps the team able to fight? |
| Flex | What problem still needs solving? |

This lineup is a starting point. Specialized teams can use other combinations as long as they can meet the demands of the challenge.

## Look for abilities that work together

Read your damage dealer's abilities before choosing teammates. A CRIT bonus can be excellent for a character who deals critical damage. Its value can vary for a character who relies on a different mechanic. A support who applies an effect the team rarely uses may contribute less than a simpler, more consistent option.

## Manage Skill Points

Basic ATKs usually help restore Skill Points, while many Skills consume them. Say your damage dealer uses a Skill every turn and two supports do the same. If the fourth member can't offset that spending, your rotation may run out of points. Alternate actions or swap in a character who can fill the same role while spending fewer points.

## Adapt your team to the enemy

Before a fight, check enemy weaknesses, the number of targets, and any dangerous effects. A lineup that's excellent against a lone boss may be less effective against several waves of enemies. Start by changing the flex slot and see how the lineup performs.

## Diagnose a tough fight

If the team goes down early, improve sustain. If the team survives but falls short on damage, check whether the bonuses help your damage dealer and whether their gear is suitable. If the rotation stalls, look at the order of actions and how many Skill Points are available.

## Do I need limited characters?

There is no universal list of must-have characters. Use the characters you own and compare how they perform in the same activity. Weigh their current abilities, your resources, and your preferences alongside any tier-list rankings.
