Choose a weapon whose effect fits what the character does in combat. Before using materials, open its details and decide whether you need more levels, Ascension, or a stronger passive from Refinement.

## Decide which upgrade solves the problem

| Action | What changes | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level up | The stats shown on the weapon screen. | Enhancement Ore and Mora. |
| Ascend | The weapon can advance beyond its current level cap. | The correct Domain, material schedule, and enemy drops. |
| Refine | The passive effect listed in the description. | The required material and whether you need another usable copy. |

For example, an effect that triggers only when you open a fight may end before the key part of your rotation. Compare it with a weapon whose passive is active during the main damage window. Keep the opponent and team the same when testing.

## Prepare the next Ascension

Open the required material’s details and note the Domain day and routes for common enemies. Subtract materials already in your inventory, including event rewards you collected. Domains supply Weapon Ascension Materials, while forging and expeditions can provide Enhancement Ore. Check the [banner calendar](/en/banner-calendar/) for announced featured weapons.
