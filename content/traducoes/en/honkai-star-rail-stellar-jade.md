Stellar Jade is an important resource if you're looking to get characters or Light Cones. Plan around the sources actually available to your account, since the amount you can earn each month varies.

## Permanent rewards

Missions, exploration, achievements, and first-clear rewards can all offer Stellar Jade. Check the rewards panel before starting an activity. If you're returning after a break, start with the missions you've already unlocked and the maps you left unfinished.

## Recurring rewards

Daily Training and certain game modes offer recurring rewards. Check the in-game list and see when those rewards reset. A short routine can be more useful than trying to complete every challenge in one day. Prioritize activities your team can handle at its current strength.

## Events and redemption codes

Pay attention to limited-time events, since their rewards may become unavailable. Read the official notices and check the end date, server, and participation requirements. For codes, use the [official redemption site](https://hsr.hoyoverse.com/gift). Never give your password to sites that promise unlimited Stellar Jade.

## Set up a simple spreadsheet

| Source | Already claimed? | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Missions | Check | Open the mission log |
| Exploration | Check | Revisit unfinished maps |
| Training | Check | Review the rewards |
| Events | Check | Read the deadline in the official notice |

Enter the amounts shown in your game. Estimates from another account may differ. Add up only the rewards you haven't claimed yet so you don't count the same achievement twice.

## Is Stellar Jade worth spending to replenish Trailblaze Power?

If you're saving for a character, spending Stellar Jade to replenish Trailblaze Power leaves you with less for Warps. We recommend treating those as separate goals and using the energy resources you already have first. Check the cost on screen before confirming any refill.

## How much can you save per month?

It depends on the version, the events, the activities you've unlocked, and which challenges you can complete. The monthly amount varies by account, with no universal guarantee. Calculate your budget using the current amounts in your inventory and revisit it when new events are announced.
