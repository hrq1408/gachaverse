Read the Echo's Cost, main stat, and Sonata Effect requirements. Compare them on the Resonator who will equip the piece. The same Echo can suit one character's role while adding little to another.

## What to check first

1. Check the Cost limit on the equipment screen and make sure the piece fits.
2. Read the main stat and decide whether it supports the character's role.
3. Check what triggers the set effect and who receives it.
4. Upgrade in stages and reassess before spending the rest of your materials.

## An example with an Outro Skill

One Sonata Effect documented by Kuro benefits the incoming Resonator when the outgoing Resonator casts an Outro Skill and the incoming Resonator casts an Intro Skill. If you plan to switch straight to your damage dealer, check that this is the order you actually use. Switching to a third teammate first can give the benefit to a different character.

Pact of Neonlight Leap is an official example of a set that depends on the outgoing and incoming Resonators. When comparing options, check both who triggers the effect and whether your switch order delivers it to the intended character.

The update notes also describe staged Echo upgrades. Use the options available in the game to stop investing when the piece stops meeting your goal. See the [Wuthering Waves hub in Portuguese](/jogos/wuthering-waves/) for related resources.
