The first deadline on December 3 is for Sigrika and Qiuyuan. Kuro Games lists **9:59 a.m. server time** as the end of their banners. The Lollo campaign and the combat event begin at **10 a.m.**, but their deadlines are different. Check the in-game countdown before making plans in your local time zone.

## Dates to keep handy

| Content | Announced start | Announced end |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sigrika, Qiuyuan, and Phase I featured weapon banners | Version 3.8 update | Dec. 3, 9:59 a.m. |
| Lollo Campaign: Groundwork | Dec. 3, 10 a.m. | Dec. 17, 3:59 a.m. |
| Dreamfield Odyssey combat event | Dec. 3, 10 a.m. | Dec. 21, 3:59 a.m. |
| Whither the Wind login event | Dec. 7, 4 a.m. | Dec. 21, 3:59 a.m. |

All dates are in 2026, and all times are server times. See the [banner calendar](/en/banner-calendar/) for other confirmed windows.

## What to prioritize in each event

Lollo commissions award stamps that can be exchanged for special deliveries. The campaign ends earlier than the combat event, so check your commissions and stamp balance first if you plan to participate in both.

Kuro Games ties a title to participation in the combat event. That is a separate goal from completing the Lollo commissions. For the December 7 login event, open the event screen to claim the daily reward. The patch notes specify claiming it through the event interface.

## If you are choosing between a Resonator and a weapon

The two banner families have separate guarantee counts. The announcement says the Featured Resonator Convene count carries across featured Resonator banners, and the Featured Weapon Convene count carries across featured weapon banners. Check which family you have made attempts in before spending resources. The end of Phase I alone does not confirm other banner dates.
