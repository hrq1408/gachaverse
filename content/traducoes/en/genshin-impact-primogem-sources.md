Start with the Primogems in your account. Keep possible rewards in another column, and mark the ones you can reasonably claim before a banner closes.

## Check the sources your account can access

| Source | How to use it in your plan |
| --- | --- |
| Commissions | Check the tasks available when you log in. |
| Story, quests, and exploration | Group nearby objectives. Open chests and unlock waypoints along your route. |
| Achievements and Domains | Check which rewards you still have to claim. |
| Events and codes | Confirm access, server deadlines, and whether you already redeemed the reward. |

## Plan around the deadline

If you can only play on the weekend and an event ends on Friday, leave that reward out of the budget. A permanent quest may still fit, but count its rewards in your balance after claiming them. Leave unannounced events out of the estimate.

Check the [banner calendar](/en/banner-calendar/) and [Genshin Impact redemption codes](/en/codes/genshin-impact/) before relying on a reward. Use the [redemption guide in Portuguese](/artigos/onde-resgatar-codigos/) to find the official channels.
