Plan character Ascension and Talent upgrades as separate lists. A character may have the materials for their next level while a particular Talent is held up by a weekly reward.

## Make a material plan

| Source | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Talent Domains | Find the day when the right book series is available. |
| Common enemies | Use the Adventurer Handbook and group nearby targets. |
| Boss and weekly rewards | Check which material and quantity you need for the next level. |
| Mora | Add up both upgrade lists before spending on another character or weapon. |

## Upgrade the ability that matters most

Check the next level of the ability that defines the character’s role. If they only enter the field to use an Elemental Skill and swap out, check that skill before spending on attacks they rarely use.

If you have books for two levels but lack the weekly drop for the second, complete the first and mark the missing drop. Use the rest of the session on an accessible Ascension. When you are short one book, check the crafting bench: lower-rarity copies of the same material can be combined. Find more progression guides in the [Genshin Impact hub](/en/games/genshin-impact/).
