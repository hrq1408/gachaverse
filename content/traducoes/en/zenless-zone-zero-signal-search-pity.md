The official event details distinguish the two exclusive Agent channels from the two W-Engine channels. Channels of the same type share the counter. Each type keeps a separate counter.

## How to check the correct history

1. Open Signal Search and identify whether the banner offers Agents or W-Engines.
2. Check the channel details for the relevant banner family.
3. Open the matching history and note the previous S-Rank result and the searches that followed it.
4. Check the featured conditions for the next result separately.

For example, if you made 20 searches since your last S-Rank on an Exclusive Channel, switching to another Exclusive Channel retains the counter. Opening a W-Engine Channel does not carry those 20 searches over. This is a hypothetical account.

## Customizing the pool and checking the guarantee

The referenced Version 3.3 announcement describes selecting six non-featured S-Rank results in eligible channels. That changes which results can appear when you miss the featured one. Check the channel details for the separate featured guarantee, and read any special channel’s own conditions before spending.
