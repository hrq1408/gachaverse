W-Engines are called Motores-W in Portuguese materials. Start with the gear description and the role you want your Agent to fill. The best first investment is the one that improves your performance in the content you're trying to clear.

## What to compare on each piece of gear

| Gear | What to check before spending |
| --- | --- |
| W-Engine | The listed Specialty, the condition for its effect, and whether your rotation meets that condition. |
| Drive Disc | The disc's stat, the set effect, and the wording that explains when the bonus applies. |
| Set swap | Which effects you actually lose and which stat gain makes the swap worthwhile. |

## Run a controlled test

If an effect depends on an Anomaly, choose a fight where you can see when the condition is met and which Agent triggers it. Swap one piece at a time. If you change the team alongside the gear, it's hard to tell which change improved the fight.

**Example.** A W-Engine has an appealing effect, but its description lists a condition your Agent doesn't meet. Compare it with a compatible W-Engine that activates during your main attack, and keep the same Drive Discs for the test.

HoYoLAB's guide tool brings together stats for Agents, Drive Discs, and W-Engines. To keep costs as low as possible, compare an item you already own with the recommended option and read the condition in the game client.

The [Zenless Zone Zero calendar](/jogos/zenless-zone-zero/calendario/) lists featured W-Engines and points you to the channel rules.
