Claim Hollow Zero's weekly rewards before the next reset. HoYoverse says expired weekly rewards cannot be reissued. A good time to check for rewards is right after a session in which you finished your tasks.

## Weekly rewards and Polychrome progress

The Version 2.6 notes describe **Reward Progress**, which tracks Polychrome rewards across Hollow Zero modes and missions, including Lost Void, Withered Domain, and The Mystery of Arpeggio Fault. Use that screen to see what is still available and check the weekly rewards separately.

| What you want to check | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| A completed weekly reward that you still need to claim | The Hollow Zero weekly rewards or commissions screen. Look for the claim indicator and the time remaining. |
| Polychrome rewards you can still earn from modes and missions | Hollow Zero's Reward Progress screen. Open the relevant entry and check its requirements. |

## Run this checklist at the end of your session

1. Return to the Hollow Zero menu after the activity.
2. Open the weekly list and look for rewards that are ready to claim.
3. Claim them and check that the interface marks them as collected.
4. To plan your next session, open Reward Progress and note activities that still have Polychromes available.

If you will only play again after the next reset, claim completed weekly rewards before planning more missions. Use the deadline displayed for your server in the game.

## What if the deadline has passed?

The HoYoverse Help Center says expired weekly Hollow Zero rewards cannot be reissued. If a reward still looks available, check whether it belongs to the current week or appears in a different progress category. The Portuguese [Polychrome guide](/artigos/zenless-zone-zero-policromos/) covers other sources and the [codes page](/jogos/zenless-zone-zero/codigos/) covers separate promotions.
