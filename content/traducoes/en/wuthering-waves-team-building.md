Build your team around the order in which its members benefit from swapping. When you meet the Concerto Energy requirement, a swap can trigger the outgoing Resonator’s Outro Skill and the next Resonator’s Intro Skill. Check who receives the effect and what makes the buff expire.

## Plan the sequence in three steps

1. Start with the Resonator who sets up the fight and keep an eye on the Concerto gauge.
2. As soon as the condition is met, swap to the Resonator who should receive the effect.
3. Check whether the effect survives another swap or ends when the recipient leaves the field.

**Example.** If the description says the buff applies to the incoming character and expires when that character leaves the field, swap directly to your damage dealer. If a third character comes in first, they may receive the buff and it may end early.

## Practice with the same lineup

Choose the same encounter and compare two swap orders. Watch how often the Intro Skill triggers and who makes use of the window. According to the official combat material, attacks and Echo Skills help generate Concerto Energy. Use the in-game gauge to decide when to swap instead of relying on a fixed number of hits.

The official description distinguishes the trigger for the swap skills from the effect applied to the next Resonator. Before testing the sequence, write down who each skill is meant to benefit and use the Concerto gauge to tell when you’re ready. The number of hits can vary.
