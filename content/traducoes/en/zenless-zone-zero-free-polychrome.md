Start by checking which rewards you can still claim. The Beginner’s Guide announcement lists Polychrome among its step rewards, and Version 2.8 describes a Polychrome guide in the Events tab. Check the current in-game interface to see what’s available on your account.

## Sort sources by deadline

| Source | What to check before counting it |
| --- | --- |
| Beginner’s Guide | Which steps are still incomplete, and which rewards have you already claimed? |
| Events | Can you meet the level and mission requirements before the event ends? |
| Codes | Does the source list the reward, and is the code still within its redemption period? |

## Estimate your balance without double-counting

Write down your available Polychrome on one line and pending rewards on another. If you’ve finished a mission but haven’t claimed its reward, move the amount to your available balance as soon as you receive it. Cross off any rewards from events you won’t be able to unlock.

**Example.** If an event requires story progress and your next session will be short, check the Beginner’s Guide steps first. They may offer another way to earn Polychrome without that deadline.

Check the [codes under review and available codes](/jogos/zenless-zone-zero/codigos/) and confirm the banner date in the [calendar](/jogos/zenless-zone-zero/calendario/).

Look for the Polychrome guide in the Events tab when it’s available. Check the reward, then read the participation requirements. An event can appear on screen before your account qualifies to claim its Polychrome.

See the [redemption guide for all four games](/artigos/onde-resgatar-codigos/) to find the official channels.
