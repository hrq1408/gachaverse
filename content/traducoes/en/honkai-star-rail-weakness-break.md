In Honkai: Star Rail, dealing damage and reducing an enemy’s Toughness are two related combat mechanics. Knowing when to trigger a Weakness Break can help you control the fight and create better openings to attack.

## What the Toughness bar shows

The HP bar shows how close you are to defeating the target. The Toughness bar shows how much Toughness you still need to reduce to trigger a Weakness Break. Under normal conditions, attacks that match one of the enemy’s weaknesses reduce its Toughness. Special abilities and rules can change how this works.

## The seven elements

| Element | Effect associated with a standard Weakness Break |
|---|---|
| Physical | Bleed |
| Fire | Burn |
| Ice | Frozen |
| Lightning | Shock |
| Wind | Wind Shear |
| Quantum | Delays the enemy’s action and can deal additional Quantum DMG |
| Imaginary | Imprisonment |

The duration and strength of these effects depend on the combat rules and any special effects in play. Check the descriptions shown in the game.

## How to plan a Weakness Break

Watch the action order and how much Toughness each enemy has left. If an enemy is preparing a dangerous attack, it may be more useful to reduce its Toughness then than to spend a powerful ability on another target. If your team relies on a specific break effect, consider which character will remove the last of the enemy’s Toughness.

## What Break Effect does

The Break Effect stat is tied to the effects of a Weakness Break. CRIT Rate and Toughness reduction are separate mechanics. Specialized abilities may follow additional rules. Before choosing Relics, check whether the character benefits from the mechanic.

## Example

Imagine two enemies. One is close to a Weakness Break and is preparing a dangerous action. The other has low HP. Compare the risk each one poses. It may be worth breaking the first enemy to control the fight, then using a simple attack on a later action to finish the second.

## Common mistakes

Avoid assuming that every attack that matches an enemy weakness produces the same effect. Pay attention to enemies protected by special mechanics. And before replacing a team that already works just to cover more elements, consider how well the characters work together and whether they can survive.
