Look at the element already on the target before your next hit. One skill may apply an aura, while the next one triggers the reaction. In a rotation, check whether that aura survives until the next attack.

## Set up a repeatable test

1. Choose an enemy with no visible aura. Avoid rain, shallow water, and other sources of elements.
2. Apply one element and check the icon above the enemy.
3. Apply a second element and note the reaction label on screen.
4. Reverse the order and compare the label, the icon, and the next application.

Pick a reaction you have already unlocked in the in-game tutorials. Record the element present before the hit and the character who landed it. Use this exercise to compare the sequence. Damage and duration vary with the fight.

## If the reaction stops happening

Reduce the rotation to two skills. Check whether the target moved, a third element was applied, or the original aura disappeared. Add one skill back at a time. Look up an unfamiliar reaction in the in-game tutorials or browse the [Genshin Impact guides](/en/games/genshin-impact/).
