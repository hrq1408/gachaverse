Light Cones strengthen characters, but rarity alone does not determine their value. Look at the passive effect and how it fits the character's abilities.

## Check the Path before equipping

A character can receive the base stats of a Light Cone from another Path, but cannot use the Path-specific passive. Start with compatible Light Cones before comparing stats. The equipment description tells you what is required.

## Evaluate the passive effect

Read the passive as a combat rule. What triggers the bonus, how long does it last, and who benefits? A passive tied to an action your character rarely takes may offer little in practice. Simpler gear can provide a more consistent effect.

## Leveling and Superimposition

Raising a Light Cone's level improves its base stats. Superimposition improves its ability. Before using a duplicate, check whether another character could use it. Treat using a duplicate as a permanent decision.

## Compare two options

| --- | --- | --- |
| Compatible Path | Check | Check |
| Useful effect | Check | Check |
| Easy to trigger | Check | Check |
| Materials required | Check | Check |

Use the Light Cones you actually own. If both fill the same role, test the one you already upgraded before committing resources to a replacement.

## Do you need a character's signature Light Cone?

Consider whether your current Light Cone lets you carry out your plan and whether the signature cone solves a specific problem. If you are saving free resources, finishing a team can provide more value than buying one isolated upgrade. The decision depends on the character's abilities and your inventory.

## Before spending materials

Lock important gear to avoid discarding it by mistake and check duplicates and material costs. If you are deciding between the Light Cone and a character ability, prioritize the change that helps your combat rotation now.
