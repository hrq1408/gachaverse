The two Character Event Wishes share their pity counter and featured guarantee. Weapon Event Wish uses a separate counter. Open the history for the right category and count the Wishes made after the last 5-star.

## Keep three concepts separate

| Concept | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Pity | Wishes since the last 5-star in that category. |
| Featured guarantee | Whether the last result gives you the next featured result under the current rules. |
| Weapon path | The selected weapon and the banner’s rules for changing or resetting the path. |

HoYoverse support gives limits of 90 for the next character 5-star and 80 for the next weapon 5-star. These are limits for the next rarity result. Check Epitomized Path under **Details** for weapons, and check the current featured rules for characters before assuming a probability or budgeting for a specific result.

## Example of switching banners

If you made 52 Wishes in Character Event Wish after the last 5-star, at most 38 remain until the next 5-star in that category. The counter carries between the two simultaneous Character Event Wishes. It does not carry to Weapon Event Wish.

Check the full event name in **Details**. Read special formats such as Chronicled Wish separately.
