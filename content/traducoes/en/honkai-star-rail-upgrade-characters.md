Upgrading a character involves more than raising their level. Many characters use the same materials, so set upgrade goals and check the results before spending more.

## Start with the character's role

A damage dealer needs to fill their offensive role, a sustain character needs to keep the team alive, and a support needs to apply their effects at the right time. Identify the character's role before deciding which stat or ability to improve.

## Levels and Ascension

Leveling improves base stats, and Ascension unlocks further stages of character progression. Check the requirements for the character you've selected and keep your account level and individual character levels in mind. If an activity is still locked, progress through the required missions so your material farming has a clear purpose.

## Traces and ability priorities

Abilities vary in importance from one rotation to another. Read what the next levels do and identify the upgrades that make a meaningful difference to the character's role. A Basic ATK you rarely use can wait, while an ability that's essential for protection or damage may deserve immediate attention.

## Check the material planning tools

Use the planning tools available in your installed version. Compare the materials you need with what you already have before you start farming.

## Example of a material budget

Pick one goal for each character on the team. For the damage dealer, choose a core ability. For the support, pick an upgrade that gives the team a greater benefit. For the sustain character, aim to get the team through the current challenge. If a goal costs more than you have available, start with the upgrade that lets you progress.

## Focus your materials on a core team

Keep a priority team and set some materials aside. When you get a new character, decide where they'll fit and which resources you can share. Keep a lineup that's working for you even if a newly released character looks stronger.

## When have you upgraded enough?

Once the character consistently fulfills their role in the content you want to clear, move on to the next bottleneck. Maxing everything out can be a personal goal. You can progress through individual stages without doing it.
